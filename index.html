FAQ

A few things to know about T1D Match.

Who is T1D Match for?

T1D Match is designed around adults living with Type 1 Diabetes who want meaningful connections with people who understand the T1D experience.

Is T1D Match a medical service?

No. T1D Match is a dating and community concept, not a medical service.
